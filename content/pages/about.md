## About Me

I'm a master's student in machine learning at the [University of Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/) and a [Zuse School ELIZA](https://eliza.school/) scholarship holder. I am writing my thesis in [Philipp Hennig's group](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/fakultaeten/mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/fachbereiche/informatik/lehrstuehle/methods-of-machine-learning/start/) and will be joining the lab as an [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/) and [IMPRS-IS](https://imprs.is.mpg.de/) PhD student in  the fall. My research interests involve understanding machine learning algorithms using probabilistic methods. More specifically, I am interested in the following topics:

- Uncertainty quantification
- Score-based generative models
- Information-theoretic perspectives for machine learning

While these are my current research interests, I am also happy to learn or chat about anything machine learning or math-related. Outside of research, I enjoy football/soccer, running, skiing, many other sports, spending time with friends, reading, and video games.
