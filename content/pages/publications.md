			<h3>Explaining Random Forests using Smoother Weights</h3>
			<p class="entry-meta"><b>K. Lüdemann</b>, R. Bhattacharjee, and U. von Luxburg</p>
			<p class="entry-meta">Zuse Schools 3rd Anniversary Poster Exhibition, 2025</p>
			<p>I propose and investigate feature importance scores for random forests from their smoother weights. The scores are based on the marginal distributions of feature values implied by reweighting the data according to the smoother weights. I then compare these scores to baseline explanation methods.</p>
			<a class="entry-link" href="./theme/documents/ELIZA_Luedemann_ZuseMeeting25.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Poster</a>
