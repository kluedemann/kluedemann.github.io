About Me

I'm a master's student in machine learning at the University of Tübingen and a Zuse School ELIZA scholarship holder. I am writing my thesis in Philipp Hennig's group and will be joining the lab as an ELLIS and IMPRS-IS PhD student in the fall. My research interests involve understanding machine learning algorithms using probabilistic methods. More specifically, I am interested in the following topics:

  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Score-based generative models
  • Information-theoretic perspectives for machine learning

While these are my current research interests, I am also happy to learn or chat about anything machine learning or math-related. Outside of research, I enjoy football/soccer, running, skiing, many other sports, spending time with friends, reading, and video games.
